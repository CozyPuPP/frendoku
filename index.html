Frendoku

Nine random frens from a roster of twenty-one. Every row, column and 3 × 3 box needs one of each.

Place real frens

Dotted clues are locked. Red marks a conflict or a mistake found by Check. A palette check means exactly nine copies are on the board, not necessarily correct.

Notes use fixed 3 × 3 positions. Impossible notes are blocked. Placing a fren clears that cell’s notes and removes it from peer notes. Erasing or replacing a placed fren restores the notes that placement removed, where they’re still valid. 1–9 follows palette order; N toggles Notes; Delete erases.

Shuffle frens changes faces only, keeping your entries and notes. Reset keeps the same puzzle and lineup. Every puzzle has one solution; Easy / Medium / Hard target 40 / 32 / 26 clues, with extra clues kept when needed for uniqueness.
